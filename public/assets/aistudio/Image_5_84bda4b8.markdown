## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-conversion, architectural D2C interface for modern streetwear and custom garment printing. The visual language intersects Japanese structural minimalism with the stark, deliberate framing of contemporary editorial fashion.

### Core Tenets
- **Quiet Precision:** Utilitarian clarity derived from rigorous spacing, monospaced metadata accents, and structured typographic locks.
- **Controlled Velocity:** Kinetic energy is driven not by ornamental gradients, but by high-contrast primary accents (`#FF5A36`) against pristine neutral foundations.
- **Tactile Materiality:** Interfaces mimic dense cotton, architectural lookbooks, and high-spec industrial garment labeling.

### Aesthetics & Tone
The design embraces a refined Minimalist-Utilitarian style. Surfaces are planar, clean, and disciplined. Layouts rely on subtle hairline separations (`#E5E5E0`), generous breathing room, and strict alignment to reinforce premium craftsmanship without sacrificing transactional frictionlessness.

## Colors

The palette employs a high-contrast architectural distribution. The canvas allows photography and textile details to lead, while functional signals remain unambiguous.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#FF5A36`):** Electric Vermilion. Reserved exclusively for direct purchase actions, live cart badges, selected states, and pivotal micro-interactions.
- **Secondary / Ink (`#171717`):** Deep carbon black. Used for primary headlines, high-priority UI labels, and solid dark button treatments.
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFAF8`):** Off-white base. Warmer than raw digital white, mitigating eye strain while evoking premium unbleached stock.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` & `#F4F4F0`):** Pure white for elevated floating cards; warm neutral tint for structured module backdrops and table cells.
- **Muted Slates (`#777777` & `#999996`):** Secondary product specs, SKU tracking, INR taxes, and inactive boundary lines.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E5E5E0`):** Ultra-crisp 1px borders providing structure without visual bulk.

### Functional States
- **Success:** `#1B8755` (Subtle botanical green, muted for streetwear refinement).
- **Critical / Error:** `#D92D20` (Precise scarlet for validation alerts).
- **Focus Rings:** 2px solid `#FF5A36` with a 2px offset in `#FAFAF8`.

## Typography

Typography establishes an editorial hierarchy with distinct roles for impact, legibility, and technical information:

- **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Employs tight tracking and deliberate scale steps. Conveys athletic authority and fashion-forward geometric presence.
- **Body & Product Attributes (Inter):** Neutral, utilitarian, and optimized for screen legibility across multi-line garment specs and sizing guides.
- **Price Architecture:** Currency prefixes (₹ / INR) are set in matching weight to numerals but scaled down 15% using `vertical-align: baseline` to avoid visual clutter.
- **Metadata Badges (`label-caps`):** Text transform is strictly uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.12em`) to mirror luxury wash-care tags and apparel spec sheets.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout balances transactional density with expansive, lookbook-style margins.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Max canvas container is 1440px centered, flanked by dynamic margins (minimum 4rem). Product detail pages run on an asymmetric 7:5 split (7 columns gallery, 5 columns sticky purchase stack).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Standard 2rem margins, 1.25rem gutters. Product feeds reflow into 2 or 3-column matrices.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** 1rem side margins with a edge-bleed mode for imagery. Full-width sticky checkout actions pin to the viewport bottom.

### Rhythm Principles
- Component internals conform strictly to `space-xs` (8px), `space-md` (16px), and `space-lg` (24px).
- Section breaks leverage generous spacing (`space-2xl` and `space-3xl`) to reproduce the pacing of a gallery monograph.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system minimizes atmospheric blur, preferring flat architecture reinforced by structured boundaries and tonal shifts.

### Depth Layers
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Flat `#FAFAF8`. No shadow.
- **Level 1 (Structural Modules / Product Tiles):** Flat `#FFFFFF` encased in a 1px solid border (`#E5E5E0`). Depth is communicated through contrast, not drop-shadows.
- **Level 2 (Hover / Active Focus):** 1px border transitions from `#E5E5E0` to `#171717`, accompanied by an ultra-diffused micro-elevation:
  `box-shadow: 0 4px 12px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav & Drawers):** Cart overlays and floating filtration drawers use frosted glass containment:
  `background: rgba(250, 250, 248, 0.88)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`, bordered by `#E5E5E0`.
- **Level 4 (Modal Dialogs):** Sheet modals use a crisp boundary with a directional floor cast:
  `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -15px rgba(23, 23, 23, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The geometric signature is disciplined and structured:

- **Micro-Radii (0.25rem / 4px):** Applied to primary cards, input containers, product thumbnails, and button blocks. This small curve softens the hard digital edge while keeping the silhouette crisp and architectural.
- **Pill Exception (Full Round / 9999px):** Applied selectively to status chips, lookbook tags, and stock counter badges to set micro-metadata apart from structural page frames.
- **Zero-Radius Elements:** Image gallery main viewers and full-width promo ribbons drop roundedness to `0px` to maintain high-fashion bleed.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA ("Add to Bag" / "Checkout"):** High-impact `#FF5A36` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Height is 52px (desktop) or 48px (mobile). Font is `Plus Jakarta Sans` 600. Shape uses `rounded-sm` (4px). Hover state deepens slightly to `#E84A27`.
- **Secondary ("Custom Print Suite" / "Quick Add"):** Solid `#171717` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Clean transition to `#333333` on hover.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** Transparent background, 1px `#E5E5E0` border, `#171717` text. On hover: border color changes to `#171717`.

### Product Cards
- Built on a pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) with a 1px `#E5E5E0` hairline outline.
- Image ratio is strictly 3:4.
- Bottom details are structured into a 2-tier row: Product title and edition number on the left (`body-sm`); Price in bold `price-md` on the right with formatted INR (`₹2,499`).
- Custom printing badge (if applicable) floats top-left: `label-caps` in a pure black pill with white text.

### Swatches & Size Selectors
- **Color Swatches:** Circular 28px diameter disks enclosed in a 1px border. Selected state adds a 2px outer ring in `#171717` separated by a 2px whitespace gap.
- **Size Chips:** Structured square buttons (44px × 44px), `rounded-sm`. Default state has a 1px `#E5E5E0` outline. Active selected state uses a solid `#171717` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Out-of-stock sizes have `#777777` text and a diagonal 1px strike-through line.

### Form Inputs
- Single-line inputs and text areas feature `#FFFFFF` surfaces with 1px `#E5E5E0` borders, padding of 14px 16px, and `rounded-sm` corners.
- Focus state switches the border directly to 1.5px `#171717` (or `#FF5A36` in checkout flows) without glowing halo rings.
- Floating helper labels use `label-caps` tracking.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Checkboxes are 18px × 18px squares with a 2px micro-radius. Checked state is solid `#FF5A36` with a crisp white tick mark.
- Radio buttons feature concentric rings: an active `#FF5A36` outer stroke and an inner `#FF5A36` center dot over a pure white backing.

### Custom Print Uploader & Sticky Action Bar
- **Upload Dropzone:** 1px dashed `#777777` over `#F4F4F0` background. Displays minimum DPI specs, accepted vectors (`SVG, AI, High-Res PNG`), and real-time print layer preview.
- **Mobile Sticky Action Bar:** Fixed to the viewport bottom with frosted acrylic styling (`rgba(250, 250, 248, 0.95)`), housing the selected SKU size, running INR total, and the primary `#FF5A36` transaction button.
